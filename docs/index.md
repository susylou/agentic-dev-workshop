# Agentic Powered Development

> Building enterprise apps with AI — on the platform, and off it.

Welcome. This guide runs alongside the workshop. You can just **watch**, or you can
**follow along** on your own instance — every hands-on page is written so you can do
either.

## Why we're here

The developer-capacity problem, and why agentic tooling is the answer worth your time.

## What you'll do

Two ways to build with AI on ServiceNow:

- **On-platform** — the **Build Agent**, in ServiceNow Studio.
- **Off-platform** — the **ServiceNow SDK (Fluent)**, driven by an AI-enabled IDE.

## How to use this guide

- **Prompt blocks have a copy button** (top-right of the block) — grab the exact text.
- Each hands-on page ends with a **:material-check-all: Done when** checklist and an
  **:material-alert: If it breaks** note.
- Where a page offers both, use the **Follow along / Watch** tabs to pick your lane.
