# Agentic Powered Development

> Building enterprise apps with AI — on the platform, and off it.

Welcome. This guide runs alongside the workshop. You can just **watch**, or you can
**follow along** on your own instance — every hands-on page is written so you can do
either.

## Why we're here

To show you how your organisation can get from a business need to something running in
production **faster**, and with **less dependence on scarce specialist developer time**.

The shift is that the people closest to the work can now build real, working applications
themselves — while your specialists are freed up for the problems that genuinely need
them. Today you'll see both ends of that: what someone can build in the browser with no
tooling at all, and what a professional developer can build with the full toolchain. Same
platform, same governance, two very different entry points.

## What you'll do

Two ways to build with AI on ServiceNow:

- **On-platform** — the **Build Agent**, in ServiceNow Studio.
- **Off-platform** — the **ServiceNow SDK (Fluent)**, driven by an AI-enabled IDE.
